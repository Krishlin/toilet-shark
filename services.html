Drain cleaning

Slow sinks, tubs, and floor drains cabled clear - then we find out why it clogged so you're not calling again next month.

  • Two or more drains running slow at once
  • Gurgling, or a clog that keeps coming back
  • Water backing up when you run the washer

What we do: We run a cable down the drain to break through the blockage, then flush it clear. We'll also inspect to find out what caused it - hair buildup, soap scum, food waste, or mineral deposits - so you know how to prevent it next time.

Why it matters: A slow drain today is a backed-up fixture tomorrow. Catching it early means a quick $150–250 fix instead of a major cleanup.

Sewer rodding

Main-line backups and root intrusion rodded out so the whole house drains again - the job most Rockford-area homes eventually need.

  • Every drain in the house is sluggish
  • Sewage smell, or water at the floor drain
  • Older home with big trees near the line

What we do: We access your main sewer line and run a heavy-duty cable through it to break up clogs, tree roots, and debris. The whole job takes 1–2 hours, and your drains work like new again.

Why it matters: A backed-up sewer line is an emergency - sewage in your home or yard is a health hazard and expensive to fix if it gets worse. We get it flowing again before it becomes a disaster.

Toilets & backups

Overflowing, gurgling, or backing up? We clear it, reseat it right on a fresh wax ring, and leave the floor dry.

  • Toilet overflows or won't flush down
  • Water seeping around the base
  • Bubbling when a nearby drain runs

What we do: We diagnose whether it's a clog in the toilet itself or a deeper issue in your drain line. If it's the toilet, we clear it and reseat it on a fresh wax ring so it seals properly. If it's the line, we rod it clear.

Why it matters: A leaking toilet wastes water and damages floors. A running or overflowing toilet is unsanitary and can quickly become expensive if you wait.
